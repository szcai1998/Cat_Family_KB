# Visual Prompt Template

When generating images or commissioning artwork, well‑crafted prompts yield better results.  Use the template below as a guide for writing prompts for hero posters, range maps, trait illustrations and other visuals.  Adapt the parameters (subject, environment, mood, lighting, composition) to match the species and desired style.

```
**Prompt title:** {e.g. “Snow Leopard Hero Poster”}

**Subject:** {Describe the animal’s pose, expression and behaviour.  Include age/sex if relevant.}

**Environment:** {Describe the setting—biome, weather, time of day.  Include notable landscape features.}

**Mood:** {Adjectives conveying atmosphere, e.g. serene, dramatic, adventurous.}

**Lighting:** {Soft morning light, golden hour, moonlit, etc.}

**Composition:** {Perspective (eye level, aerial), framing (close‑up, full body), rule of thirds, etc.}

**Style and details:** {Realistic, painterly, impressionistic; include specific details like fur texture, colouration, background elements.}

**Narrative elements:** {Optional – suggest a story or action, such as stalking prey or resting on a rocky ledge.}

```

Use separate prompts for each visual asset.  Adjust wording to guide the AI or artist toward the desired result.  Save prompts in `04_Visual_Production/Species_Prompt_Sets` or `Prompt_Templates`.
