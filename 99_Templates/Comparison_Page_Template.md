# Comparison Page Template

Comparison pages examine similarities and differences between two or more species.  They help readers appreciate evolutionary convergence and ecological divergence.  Use this template when creating pages like “Tiger vs Lion” or “Cheetah vs Leopard”.

```
# {Comparison title}

## Introduction

Briefly introduce the species being compared and explain why this comparison is interesting (e.g. overlapping ranges, similar appearance, contrasting hunting styles).

## Morphology

Compare body size, coat patterns, sexual dimorphism and other anatomical features.  Use a table if helpful.

## Behaviour

Discuss differences in sociality, hunting strategy, daily activity patterns and vocalisations.

## Habitat and Range

Describe where each species lives and how their distributions overlap or diverge.

## Conservation Status

Summarise IUCN status, population trends and major threats for each species.  Highlight how conservation actions may differ.

## Cultural Significance

Optional section on cultural roles, myths or human perceptions.

## Conclusion

Reflect on what this comparison reveals about adaptation and evolution.

```

Cite sources for all factual statements.  Feel free to include images or diagrams to illustrate differences.
