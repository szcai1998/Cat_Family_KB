# Habitat Map Poster

This script outlines an infographic that maps the geographic ranges of the felid species covered in this knowledge base.  The poster will help readers visualise where each cat lives and how their ranges overlap or differ.

## Concept

Use a world map (or separate regional maps) with coloured overlays representing the distribution of each species.  Each overlay should be labelled with the species name and keyed to a legend.  For species with wide ranges (e.g. [[Cougar - Puma]]), show a gradient; for restricted species (e.g. [[Siberian Tiger]]), use a small shaded area.

## Layout suggestions

* Use different colours or patterns for each species overlay; include a legend matching colours to species names.
* If the map becomes cluttered, group species by region and create small inset maps (e.g. Central/South America for jaguar and ocelot; Eurasia for snow leopard, Eurasian lynx and Pallas’s cat).
* Include brief notes around the map summarising special range facts (e.g. the cougar’s wide range【623734751930192†L32-L39】, the remnant population of Asiatic lions).
