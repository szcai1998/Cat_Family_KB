# Three Leopards Poster

This script describes a poster comparing the three felids commonly called “leopards”: the **African leopard**, the **snow leopard**, and the **clouded leopard**.  Despite sharing a common name, these cats differ in genus, habitat and morphology.

## Concept

Design three panels side‑by‑side, each dedicated to one leopard.  Include an image or illustration, scientific name, sub‑family and genus, distribution map and key features.  Highlight that only the African leopard is a “true” leopard (*Panthera pardus*).

## Panels

### African Leopard (*Panthera pardus*)

- **Branch:** [[Pantherinae]]
- **Genus:** *Panthera* (true big cat)
- **Coat:** golden with small rosettes **without central dots**【103081960674531†L200-L215】.
- **Range:** sub‑Saharan Africa and parts of Asia.
- **Habitat:** forests, savannas and mountains; excellent climber.
- **Conservation:** Vulnerable.

### Snow Leopard (*Panthera uncia*)

- **Branch:** [[Pantherinae]]
- **Genus:** *Panthera*
- **Coat:** pale grey with rosettes; thick fur 5–12 cm long【195888652515351†L95-L101】; long tail.
- **Range:** high mountains of Central and South Asia at 3,000–4,500 m【195888652515351†L118-L122】.
- **Special:** cannot roar【195888652515351†L129-L131】; more closely related to tigers than to leopards【195888652515351†L138-L142】.
- **Conservation:** Vulnerable.

### Clouded Leopard (*Neofelis nebulosa*)

- **Branch:** [[Pantherinae]]
- **Genus:** *Neofelis* (ancient pantherine)
- **Coat:** large “cloud” blotches on a yellowish‑brown background; elongated canine teeth【452191132347828†L152-L182】.
- **Range:** forests of Southeast Asia.
- **Special:** cannot roar or purr; climbs head‑first down trees【452191132347828†L152-L182】.
- **Conservation:** Vulnerable.

## Design tips

* Use colours or icons to indicate each cat’s conservation status.
* Add arrows or a flow line connecting the panels to emphasise that snow leopards and clouded leopards are not true leopards despite the name.
* Include a taxonomy key explaining that only species in the genus *Panthera* are true big cats.
