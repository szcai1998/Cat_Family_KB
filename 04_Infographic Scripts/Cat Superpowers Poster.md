# Cat Superpowers Poster

This script outlines a playful infographic that showcases the **“superpowers”** of different felids—stand‑out abilities that set each species apart.

## Concept

Arrange a series of panels or icons, each representing a cat with a unique superlative.  Use short captions and stylised illustrations or icons to communicate the power at a glance.  Group powers by category (speed, strength, stealth, agility, adaptation).

## Layout ideas

* Use a comic‑style grid with bold headings for each power.
* Include a silhouette or stylised drawing of each cat performing its superpower (e.g. cheetah sprinting, serval leaping, jaguar biting through a turtle shell).
* Provide a short caption below each panel referencing the citation.
