# Cat Family Atlas Home

Welcome to the **Cat Family Atlas**, a living knowledge base about the world’s felines.  
This atlas combines taxonomy, ecology, conservation and museum‑style storytelling to help you explore the diversity of cats.  
It covers the **big cats** of the _Pantherinae_ subfamily, the **small and medium cats** of the _Felinae_ subfamily, and even a domestic breed to help you see how wild ancestors connect to house cats.  

Use the links below to navigate:

- **[[../01_Taxonomy_and_Evolution/Felidae_Overview.md|Felidae Overview]]** – learn about the evolution and classification of the cat family.  
- **[[../00_Atlas_Home/Species_Index.md|Species Index]]** – access profiles for every species in the atlas.  
- **[[../03_Exhibit_Modules/Range_and_Habitat/]]** – explore range maps and habitat modules (placeholders for future interactive exhibits).  
- **[[../05_Ecology_Comparisons/Apex_Predators.md|Ecology comparisons]]** – compare cats by hunting style, habitat, sociality and other ecological themes.  
- **[[../06_Conservation/Conservation_Status_Index.md|Conservation]]** – understand threats and conservation efforts across the cat family.  

This atlas is organised as a **museum**: each species has a **hero exhibit page** with summaries, trait highlights and conservation notes, while deeper modules provide range, habitat, diet, behaviour and scale information.  Visual production assets (posters, 3D models, maps) are represented by placeholders in the file system so you can later attach your own media.

If you are new to this atlas, read **[[../00_Atlas_Home/How_to_Use_This_KB.md|How to Use This KB]]** for guidance on navigating and extending the knowledge base.
