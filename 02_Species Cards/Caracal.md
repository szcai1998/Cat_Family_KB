# Caracal

## Scientific classification

- **Sub‑family:** [[Felinae]]
- **Genus:** *Caracal*
- **Species:** *Caracal caracal*

## Physical characteristics

Caracals are medium‑sized wildcats with a **sleek, reddish‑gold coat** and a white underside.  They have long legs and are the **heaviest and fastest of Africa’s small cats**【774389635293037†L50-L55】.  Their most striking feature is a pair of **long, black ear tufts** that may help camouflage the cat’s outline or aid communication【774389635293037†L63-L67】.  Adults weigh 8–19 kg; males are larger than females.

## Habitat and range

Caracals inhabit **savannas, semi‑deserts, dry woodlands, arid steppes and mountains** across Africa and the Middle East, reaching as far east as India【774389635293037†L70-L81】.  They are versatile and can survive in areas with sparse vegetation.  Caracals often coexist with servals and may compete with them for prey.

## Behaviour and diet

Caracals are solitary, mainly nocturnal or crepuscular hunters.  They prey on birds, small mammals, rodents and reptiles.  Using their powerful hind legs, caracals can **leap up to 10 feet (3 m)** into the air to swat birds out of flight【774389635293037†L70-L81】.  They stalk and pounce on ground‑dwelling prey and may also climb trees.  Caracals can purr and make a variety of vocalisations, including growls, hisses and meows【774389635293037†L94-L97】.

## Special traits

- **Ear tufts:** long black tufts up to 5 cm adorn the ears; they may deter insects or serve as visual signals【774389635293037†L63-L67】.
- **Spectacular leaps:** caracals can jump vertically to catch birds in mid‑air【774389635293037†L70-L81】.
- **Historical association:** in India and Persia caracals were trained to catch birds in royal hunts; this practice gave rise to the phrase “to put a cat among the pigeons”【774389635293037†L115-L121】.

## Conservation status

Caracals are listed as **Least Concern**, though Asian populations are declining due to habitat loss and persecution.  They sometimes prey on livestock, leading to human conflict【774389635293037†L123-L129】.
