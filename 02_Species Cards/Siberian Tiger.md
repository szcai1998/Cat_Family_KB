# Siberian Tiger (Amur Tiger)

## Scientific classification

- **Sub‑family:** [[Pantherinae]]
- **Genus:** *Panthera*
- **Species:** *Panthera tigris altaica*

## Physical characteristics

Siberian (or Amur) tigers are the **largest living cats**.  Males can reach lengths of over 10 feet (about 3 m) from nose to tail and weigh up to **660 pounds (300 kg)**【736746662787535†L160-L166】.  They have thick, pale‑orange fur with narrow black stripes and a dense layer of fat to insulate them from harsh winter temperatures.  Their large paws act like snowshoes, spreading their weight over soft snow.

## Habitat and range

These tigers inhabit the **birch forests of the Russian Far East**, primarily in the Amur‑Ussuri region.  A few individuals also occur across the border in northeastern China and possibly North Korea【259955699961623†L75-L81】.  The subspecies is adapted to cold climates and travels long distances through mountainous terrain.

## Behaviour and diet

Siberian tigers are solitary ambush predators.  They use their camouflaged coat and powerful limbs to stalk and pounce on prey such as deer and wild boar.  Tigers are strong swimmers and will enter rivers or lakes in pursuit of prey.  Like other pantherine cats they can roar loudly to communicate over long distances.

## Special traits

- **Largest cat:** the Siberian tiger holds the record for the largest body size among living felids【736746662787535†L160-L166】.
- **Cold adaptation:** thick fur and a significant fat layer insulate them against sub‑zero temperatures.
- **Conservation icon:** despite protection, the subspecies is endangered due to habitat loss and poaching.  Conservation programmes in Russia and China aim to stabilise populations.

## Conservation status

The Siberian tiger is listed as **Endangered** on the IUCN Red List.  Although numbers increased from the brink of extinction in the 20th century, fewer than a thousand individuals remain in the wild.  Threats include logging, poaching and depletion of prey.  Transboundary cooperation between Russia and China is vital for its survival.
