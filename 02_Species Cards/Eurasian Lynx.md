# Eurasian Lynx

## Scientific classification

- **Sub‑family:** [[Felinae]]
- **Genus:** *Lynx*
- **Species:** *Lynx lynx*

## Physical characteristics

The Eurasian lynx is a medium‑sized wildcat distinguished by its **black ear tufts** and **short, stubby tail**【200188625492883†L41-L66】.  Its fur is thick and variable in colour, ranging from reddish‑brown to greyish, with spots more prominent in some individuals.  Lynxes have **long legs and large, fur‑covered paws**; these paws spread their weight like snowshoes, allowing them to walk on snow without sinking【200188625492883†L70-L74】.  Adults typically weigh 18–36 kg.

## Habitat and range

The Eurasian lynx is widely distributed across northern, central and eastern Europe and into Siberia and Central Asia.  It inhabits dense forests, boreal taiga and mountainous regions.  The species once declined severely in Western Europe but has been successfully reintroduced to parts of Germany, Switzerland and the Alps【200188625492883†L129-L142】.

## Behaviour and diet

Lynxes are solitary and elusive.  They hunt **at night**, using stealth and patience to stalk prey before making a sudden pounce【200188625492883†L81-L99】.  Their diet includes roe deer, hares, grouse and other small mammals.  Lynxes have excellent eyesight and can spot a mouse from 250 feet (76 m) away【200188625492883†L81-L99】.  They avoid humans and rely on cover to approach prey.

## Special traits

- **Ear tufts:** the lynx’s conspicuous ear tufts may enhance hearing or communication【200188625492883†L41-L66】.
- **Snowshoe paws:** wide, furred paws enable movement over deep snow【200188625492883†L70-L74】.
- **Wide distribution:** Eurasian lynx populations extend across two continents and have been successfully reintroduced in parts of Europe【200188625492883†L129-L142】.

## Conservation status

The Eurasian lynx is listed as **Least Concern**, but some local populations are threatened by habitat fragmentation, poaching and reduction of prey.  Reintroduction projects and legal protection have aided recovery in many regions.
