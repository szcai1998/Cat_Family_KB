# African Lion – Range and Habitat

## Overview

> From ocean-like grasslands to the blistering edges of the Kalahari desert, the king's domain is vast but fracturing.

The African Lion (_Panthera leo_) is the apex predator of the African savannah. Historically the most widespread land mammal after humans, its modern range tells a story of drastic decline and extreme habitat fragmentation.

## Key Information

- **Historic Range:** Spanned almost the entirety of the African continent, the Middle East, and parts of southern Europe.
- **Current Range:** Occupies approximately 1.57 million km² across sub-Saharan Africa. This represents a staggering reduction to only ~6% of their historical range.
- **Habitat Type:** Heavily favors open savannahs, grasslands, and lightly wooded scrub.
- **Terrain Preference:** Requires environments that provide enough cover for ambush hunting (tall grass, acacia thickets) while supporting high densities of large ungulate herds.

## Interpretation and Comparisons

Lions are the only big cats that thrive in open plains rather than dense forests or jungles (unlike the jaguar or tiger). Their social structure evolved specifically to hunt on the open savannah, where cooperative tactics are necessary to take down massive prey in plain sight.
