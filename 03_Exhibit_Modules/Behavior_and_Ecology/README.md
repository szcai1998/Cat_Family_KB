# Behaviour and Ecology Modules

Behaviour and ecology modules delve into social structures (solitary, pair‑bonded, or group living), communication methods (vocalisations, scent marking, visual displays), reproductive cycles, territoriality and interactions with other species. They also cover daily activity patterns (diurnal/nocturnal/crepuscular), seasonal movements and ecological roles (apex predator, mesopredator, keystone species).

Use these modules to provide a fuller picture of how each cat lives in the wild. Including observations from field research and indigenous knowledge can enrich the content.
