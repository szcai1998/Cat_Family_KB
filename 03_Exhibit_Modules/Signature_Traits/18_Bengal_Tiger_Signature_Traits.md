# Bengal Tiger – Signature Traits

## Overview

> Searing orange fire striped with midnight black, the ultimate disruptive camouflage of the tall grass.

The Bengal Tiger (_Panthera tigris tigris_) represents the quintessential image of a tiger. Its signature traits are designed specifically to hide a massive predator in plain sight.

## Key Information

- **The Coat:** They possess the brightest, most vibrant reddish-orange coat of any tiger subspecies, crossed with stark, high-contrast black stripes.
- **White Spots:** The backs of their ears feature large white spots (ocelli). These act as visual beacons, allowing cubs to follow their mother silently through the dense, dark undergrowth.
- **Unique Patterns:** Like a human fingerprint, no two tigers have the exact same stripe pattern. Researchers use these patterns to identify and track individual tigers via camera traps.

## Interpretation and Comparisons

While a bright orange coat seems like it would stand out, it is actually the perfect camouflage. Deer (their primary prey) are dichromatic, meaning they are red-green colorblind. To a deer, the tiger's searing orange coat appears as a dull, yellowish-green that perfectly matches the surrounding tall grass.
