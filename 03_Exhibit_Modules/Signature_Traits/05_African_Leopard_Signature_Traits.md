# African Leopard – Signature Traits

## Overview

> A coat of clustered rosettes that acts as the perfect optical illusion in the dappled light of the African bush.

The African Leopard (_Panthera pardus pardus_) is instantly recognizable by its striking coat, but its signature traits go far beyond its spots.

## Key Information

- **Rosettes:** The leopard's coat is covered in rosettes (clusters of black spots arranged in a circle). Unlike the jaguar, the centers of leopard rosettes are empty and do not contain central spots.
- **The Long Tail:** A leopard's tail can be over 1 meter long, often measuring more than half the length of its entire body.
- **White Ear Spots:** The back of a leopard's ears feature stark white spots (ocelli) surrounded by black hair.

## Interpretation and Comparisons

Every one of these traits serves a crucial survival function. The empty rosettes perfectly mimic the shadows cast by leaves, allowing the cat to vanish in the brush. The exceptionally long tail acts as a counterbalance, allowing the leopard to make sharp turns at high speed or walk along incredibly thin branches. Finally, the white ear spots act as "follow me" beacons for cubs navigating through tall grass at night.
