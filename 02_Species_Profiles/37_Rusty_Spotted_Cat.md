# Rusty-spotted Cat

> The hummingbird of the cat family, a pint-sized predator with oversized courage.

## 1. Core Identity

- **Common name:** Rusty-spotted Cat
- **Alternative names:** Hummingbird cat
- **Scientific name:** _Prionailurus rubiginosus_
- **Taxonomic group:** Felinae, Prionailurus
- **Lineage:** Leopard Cat lineage
- **Exhibit role:** The Micro Predator
- **Main biome:** Dry forests, scrublands, and rocky hills
- **Main hunting style:** Stalk-and-pounce

## 2. Museum Summary

Weighing less than a bag of sugar, the rusty-spotted cat easily claims the title of one of the world's smallest wild felines. Found only in India and Sri Lanka, this tiny, hyperactive hunter is a master of the underbrush. What it lacks in size, it more than makes up for in sheer ferocity and agility. It zips through the forest with lightning speed, hunting rodents, frogs, and insects, and is not afraid to stand its ground against much larger animals. Its soft, greyish coat is beautifully adorned with the namesake rust-coloured spots, providing perfect camouflage in the dry leaves.

## 3. Range

- **Continents:** Asia
- **Countries/regions:** Endemic to India, Sri Lanka, and a small population in Nepal.
- **Range type:** Patchy but widespread within its region.
- **Range notes:** Once thought to be extremely rare, camera traps have recently revealed they are more widespread than previously believed, just incredibly secretive.

## 4. Habitat

- **Primary habitat:** Dry deciduous forests, scrub, and rocky outcrops.
- **Secondary habitat:** Tropical rainforests and tea estates (particularly in Sri Lanka).
- **Climate adaptation:** Adaptable to both humid tropical environments and arid scrub.
- **Terrain preference:** Favors areas with dense ground cover or rocky crevices for hiding.

## 5. Diet

- **Main prey:** Small rodents, insects, and frogs.
- **Secondary prey:** Small birds and lizards.
- **Hunting method:** Extremely fast terrestrial stalk-and-pounce.
- **Feeding ecology:** Because of its tiny size, it must eat frequently, consuming a massive amount of insects and rodents relative to its body weight.

## 6. Behaviour / Ecology

- **Social structure:** Solitary.
- **Activity rhythm:** Strictly nocturnal (active at night).
- **Territory:** Very small home ranges.
- **Ecological role:** Crucial micro-predator controlling agricultural rodent pests.

## 7. Build & Scale

- **Body size:** 35–48 cm (14–19 in).
- **Weight range:** 0.8–1.7 kg (1.8–3.7 lbs).
- **Key proportions:** Fits easily in the palms of a human's hands; has proportionally large eyes for night vision.
- **Movement style:** Hyperactive, darting, and exceptionally fast.

## 8. Signature Traits

- **Extreme Miniaturization:** Alongside the African black-footed cat, it holds the record for the smallest wild cat. A fully grown adult male is half the size of a standard domestic cat.
- **Arboreal Refuge:** When pursued by larger predators like leopards or feral dogs, the rusty-spotted cat can bolt straight up a tree with the speed of a squirrel.
- **The "Hummingbird" Metabolism:** Its tiny size requires an incredibly fast metabolism, making it a highly active and almost constantly foraging hunter during the night.

## 9. Conservation

- **IUCN status:** Near Threatened (NT)
- **Population trend:** Decreasing.
- **Main threats:** Habitat loss due to the massive expansion of agriculture in India, and mortality from feral domestic dogs.
- **Conservation notes:** Surprisingly, they have adapted to living in abandoned agricultural fields and tea plantations, offering some hope for their coexistence alongside human development.
