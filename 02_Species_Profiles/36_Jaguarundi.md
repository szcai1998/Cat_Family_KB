# Jaguarundi

> The bizarre, weasel-like otter cat that breaks all the rules of feline aesthetics.

## 1. Core Identity

- **Common name:** Jaguarundi
- **Alternative names:** Otter cat, Eyra cat
- **Scientific name:** _Herpailurus yagouaroundi_
- **Taxonomic group:** Felinae, Herpailurus
- **Lineage:** Puma lineage
- **Exhibit role:** The Weasel Cat
- **Main biome:** Highly adaptable (rainforests, savannas, scrublands)
- **Main hunting style:** Ground pursuit

## 2. Museum Summary

At first glance, the jaguarundi hardly looks like a cat at all. With its long, slender body, short legs, flat head, and unspotted coat, it looks remarkably like a large weasel or an otter. Despite sharing a close evolutionary lineage with the massive puma, the jaguarundi has evolved a completely different body plan, optimized for slipping quickly through dense, thorny underbrush and thick grasses without getting snagged. It is an incredibly adaptable and highly vocal predator, hunting during the day and constantly communicating with a bizarre repertoire of chirps, purrs, and bird-like whistles.

## 3. Range

- **Continents:** North America, South America
- **Countries/regions:** From northern Mexico down through Central America to central Argentina.
- **Range type:** Widespread and continuous.
- **Range notes:** Historically ranged into southern Texas, though there have been no confirmed sightings in the US in recent decades.

## 4. Habitat

- **Primary habitat:** Lowland brush, thorny scrub, savannas, and forest edges.
- **Secondary habitat:** Tropical rainforests and gallery forests near water.
- **Climate adaptation:** Prefers warmer lowlands; rarely found above 2,000 meters.
- **Terrain preference:** Requires dense ground-level vegetation to utilize its low-slung body.

## 5. Diet

- **Main prey:** Small rodents, ground-dwelling birds, and reptiles (lizards, snakes).
- **Secondary prey:** Fish and frogs.
- **Hunting method:** Terrestrial pursuit; uses its weasel-like body to chase prey through thick tangles where larger predators cannot follow.
- **Feeding ecology:** Highly opportunistic, quickly shifting its diet to whatever is most abundant.

## 6. Behaviour / Ecology

- **Social structure:** Solitary, though occasionally observed hunting in pairs.
- **Activity rhythm:** Strongly diurnal (active during the day), which is unusual for small cats.
- **Territory:** Maintains relatively small home ranges that often overlap.
- **Ecological role:** Ground-level mesopredator that heavily impacts reptile and ground-bird populations.

## 7. Build & Scale

- **Body size:** 53–77 cm (21–30 in) body length.
- **Weight range:** 2.5–9 kg (5.5–20 lbs).
- **Key proportions:** Exceptionally long body, very short legs, and a small, flattened head with small, rounded ears.
- **Movement style:** Scurrying, low-to-the-ground run that closely resembles a mustelid (weasel).

## 8. Signature Traits

- **The Otter-Cat Body:** Its unique, elongated, short-legged morphology allows it to move fluidly through impenetrable thickets and brambles without snagging its fur.
- **Unspotted Coat:** Unlike almost all other small cats in the Americas, the jaguarundi has a uniform, unspotted coat. It comes in two distinct color phases: a dark grey/black (often found in dense forests) and a reddish-brown (often found in dry scrub).
- **Avian Vocalizations:** The jaguarundi has an unusually wide vocal range, featuring at least 13 distinct calls, including a bird-like chirp used for greeting and communication.

## 9. Conservation

- **IUCN status:** Least Concern (LC)
- **Population trend:** Decreasing.
- **Main threats:** Habitat loss due to large-scale agriculture and deforestation.
- **Conservation notes:** While the species as a whole is secure, the North American population (the Gulf Coast Jaguarundi) is highly endangered and protected in Mexico.

## 10. Visual Production Notes

- **Preview Card:** A reddish-brown jaguarundi peering out from a dense thicket, highlighting its flat, weasel-like head and small ears.
- **Anatomy Poster:** Contrast its long, tubular body and short legs against a standard cat silhouette.
- **Hunting Scene:** A jaguarundi sprinting low to the ground in a dusty Mexican scrubland, chasing a lizard.
- **Range Map:** Showing its vast range from Mexico to Argentina.
