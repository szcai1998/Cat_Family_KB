# African Lion

> **Social savanna apex predator**

## 1. Core Identity

- **Common name:** African lion
- **Alternative names:** Lion
- **Scientific name:** *Panthera leo*
- **Taxonomic group:** Subfamily *Pantherinae*; genus *Panthera*
- **Lineage:** Lion lineage within the Panthera clade
- **Exhibit role:** Big cat exhibit
- **Main biome:** Grasslands, savannas and open woodlands
- **Main hunting style:** Cooperative ambush and chase

## 2. Museum Summary

The **African lion** is the only truly social cat, living in groups called **prides** that can include up to three adult males, a dozen related females and their cubs【831700127756655†L15-L90】.  Lions are among the largest felids: males measure **4.5–6.5 feet** in body length and weigh **265–420 pounds**, while females are slightly smaller【831700127756655†L15-L90】.  Males possess a thick mane that signals maturity and offers protection during fights.  Lions inhabit **grasslands, savannas and open woodlands** across sub‑Saharan Africa, though they have lost over 90 % of their historic range【831700127756655†L15-L90】.  Unlike solitary cats, lionesses hunt cooperatively, bringing down large ungulates such as zebras and wildebeest, while males defend the territory【816664480957507†L190-L256】.  Their roar can be heard up to 8 km away.  African lions are listed as **Vulnerable** and their populations are declining due to habitat loss, retaliatory killing and reduced prey【831700127756655†L94-L140】【816664480957507†L190-L256】.

## 3. Range

- **Continents:** Africa (with a remnant Asiatic population in India’s Gir Forest, not covered here).
- **Countries/regions:** Patchily distributed across sub‑Saharan Africa; strongholds in eastern and southern Africa; extirpated from North Africa and parts of West Africa【831700127756655†L15-L90】.
- **Range type:** Highly fragmented; confined to protected areas and sparsely populated regions.
- **Range notes:** Lions have lost about 94 % of their historic African range【831700127756655†L15-L90】.

## 4. Habitat

- **Primary habitat:** Open grasslands and savannas with abundant prey【831700127756655†L15-L90】.
- **Secondary habitat:** Scrublands, semi‑deserts and dry thorn forests【831700127756655†L15-L90】.
- **Climate adaptation:** Lions are adapted to warm climates; they rest in the shade during the heat of the day and hunt at night or in cool hours.
- **Terrain preference:** Flat or gently rolling terrain that allows cooperative stalking and running.

## 5. Diet

- **Main prey:** Medium‑to‑large ungulates such as wildebeest, zebras and African buffalo【816664480957507†L190-L256】.
- **Secondary prey:** Smaller antelopes, warthogs, and occasionally giraffes or elephants; lions also scavenge and steal kills from other predators【816664480957507†L190-L256】.
- **Hunting method:** Cooperative hunts led by lionesses.  They stalk within striking distance and then chase prey in coordinated bursts.  Males may assist in hunting large prey.
- **Feeding ecology:** Lions gorge on kills and may rest nearby for days.  They tolerate scavengers like hyenas but will fight to defend carcasses.

## 6. Behaviour / Ecology

- **Social structure:** Organised into prides.  Females are related and remain in the natal pride; males form coalitions and may take over prides by ousting resident males【831700127756655†L15-L90】.
- **Activity rhythm:** Largely nocturnal or crepuscular, but can be active at any time when food is scarce or conditions permit.
- **Territory:** Prides defend territories ranging from 20 km² to over 400 km² depending on prey abundance; males patrol boundaries and scent‑mark.
- **Ecological role:** Apex predator and keystone species; helps regulate herbivore populations and scavenge carrion.

## 7. Build & Scale

- **Body size:** Males 4.5–6.5 ft in length; shoulder height about 3.5–4 ft【831700127756655†L15-L90】【816664480957507†L170-L189】.
- **Weight range:** Males **330–550 lb** (150–250 kg); females **265–395 lb** (120–180 kg)【816664480957507†L170-L189】.
- **Key proportions:** Males have a mane and larger head; females sleeker and more agile.
- **Movement style:** Strong sprinters over short distances; cannot sustain high speeds for long.  Good swimmers and climbers when necessary.

## 8. Signature Traits

1. **Mane:** Male lions grow a mane that offers protection and signals fitness to rivals and females.
2. **Sociality:** Only cat species that lives in large multi‑generation groups【831700127756655†L15-L90】.
3. **Powerful roar:** Can be heard over long distances and used for communication and territorial advertisement.
4. **Cooperative hunting:** Females coordinate to bring down large prey【816664480957507†L190-L256】.

## 9. Conservation

- **IUCN status:** Vulnerable.
- **Population trend:** Decreasing; an estimated **20,000** lions remain in Africa【816664480957507†L170-L189】【816664480957507†L190-L256】.
- **Main threats:** Habitat loss, loss of prey base, retaliatory killing due to livestock depredation, disease and illegal hunting【831700127756655†L94-L140】【816664480957507†L190-L256】.
- **Conservation notes:** Conservation strategies include creating and connecting protected areas, community‑based conservation, compensation schemes for livestock loss and anti‑poaching initiatives.
