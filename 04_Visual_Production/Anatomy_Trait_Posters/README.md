# Anatomy and Trait Posters

These posters focus on key anatomical or behavioural traits of each species. They might include diagrams of skulls, skeletal structures, muscle groups, paw morphology, coat patterns, or specialised organs (such as the cheetah’s enlarged nostrils and lungs). Combine illustrations with concise captions to explain how traits relate to function and ecology.

Place finished posters or placeholder files here and document their design in Markdown.
