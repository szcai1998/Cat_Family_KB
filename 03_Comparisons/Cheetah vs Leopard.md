# Cheetah vs Leopard

Cheetahs (*Acinonyx jubatus*) and leopards (*Panthera pardus*) share a spotted appearance but differ markedly in anatomy, behaviour and taxonomy.

## Taxonomic placement

- **Cheetah**: belongs to the **Felinae** sub‑family.  It cannot roar but can purr and has non‑retractable claws【281884566239886†L57-L66】.
- **Leopard**: a member of the **Pantherinae**.  It has retractable claws and can roar like other big cats【844804255495323†L246-L260】.

## Body build

- **Cheetah**: extremely slender with long legs, flexible spine and deep chest.  Built for speed; capable of sprinting up to 70 mph【281884566239886†L57-L66】.
- **Leopard**: more muscular and compact.  Built for power and agility in climbing; not as fast as the cheetah but stronger.

## Claws and movement

- **Cheetah**: **non‑retractable claws** provide traction during sprints【281884566239886†L57-L66】.  Tail acts as a stabiliser during high‑speed chases.
- **Leopard**: retractable claws allow for silent stalking and climbing.  Leopards often haul prey into trees for safekeeping.

## Coat pattern

- **Cheetah**: coat is covered with solid black spots; also features distinctive “tear marks” running from the eyes to the mouth【281884566239886†L57-L66】.
- **Leopard**: coat displays rosettes (small circular patterns) without central spots【103081960674531†L200-L215】.

## Hunting strategy

- **Cheetah**: diurnal hunter; relies on sheer speed and acceleration to run down small antelope【281884566239886†L69-L81】.
- **Leopard**: primarily nocturnal; uses stealth to stalk prey and ambushes with a short burst of speed.  Versatile diet includes medium‑sized mammals, birds and fish.
