# Cougar vs Lion

Although cougars (pumas) and lions are both large cats, they belong to different sub‑families and differ dramatically in anatomy and behaviour.

## Taxonomy and vocalisation

- **Cougar** (*Puma concolor*) – a member of the **Felinae** sub‑family.  Cougars have a rigid hyoid bone and **cannot roar**; they purr, hiss and growl【889426918678370†L42-L50】.  They are part of the Puma lineage along with the cheetah【889426918678370†L54-L67】.
- **African lion** (*Panthera leo*) – a member of the **Pantherinae** sub‑family.  Lions possess a flexible hyoid and can **roar loudly**, with roars audible up to five miles【412797920315225†L67-L97】.

## Social structure

- **Cougar**: solitary.  Adults maintain large territories and meet only to mate.  Females raise cubs alone【623734751930192†L110-L113】.
- **Lion**: social.  Lions live in **prides** consisting of related females and a coalition of males【412797920315225†L86-L104】.  Females hunt cooperatively and males defend the group.

## Body form and coat

- **Cougar**: slender body with a uniform tawny coat and a long tail with a black tip.  No mane.  Weighs up to 90 kg【889426918678370†L54-L67】.
- **Lion**: stocky build with a tawny coat.  Adult males possess a **mane** that varies in colour and length.  Males can weigh over 180 kg.

## Range and habitat

- **Cougar**: ranges from Canada to the southern Andes, occupying forests, mountains, deserts and wetlands【623734751930192†L32-L39】.
- **Lion**: today confined mainly to **sub‑Saharan Africa**, inhabiting savannas and open woodlands.  A small Asiatic population survives in India’s Gir Forest.

## Prey and hunting strategy

- **Cougar**: ambush predator that feeds primarily on deer and other ungulates, supplemented by smaller mammals.  Utilises stealth and powerful hind legs for pouncing.
- **Lion**: cooperative hunter; prides prey on medium‑ to large‑sized ungulates such as zebras and wildebeest.  Lions will scavenge and steal kills from other predators.
