# Master Cat List (Felidae Family Index)

Welcome to the **Felidae knowledge base**.  The family Felidae contains all of the living cat species.  Scientists divide this family into two sub‑families: **Pantherinae** and **Felinae**.  Pantherinae contains the large cats that can roar, such as tigers, lions, jaguars and leopards.  Felinae includes the remaining small‑to‑medium cats, from domestic cats and cheetahs to cougars, lynxes and other lesser known species【844804255495323†L246-L260】.  Biologists estimate there are 14 extant genera and about 45 species of felids【844804255495323†L318-L324】.  The earliest split between Pantherinae and Felinae occurred more than 10 million years ago【844804255495323†L266-L279】.

This index lists the species covered in this knowledge base.  Each row links to a detailed page about a cat, along with its sub‑family and a short description.  Use these links to navigate the wiki. ### How to use this wiki

* **Explore species pages** – click a name in the table to open its dedicated page where you’ll find classification, physical characteristics, habitat, behaviour, special traits and conservation notes, all with citations.
* **Learn the taxonomy** – the taxonomy section explains how the cat family is organised and why some cats can roar while others can only purr.
* **Compare cats** – the comparisons section examines differences between similar species (e.g. leopard vs jaguar) and between the big‑cat and small‑cat branches.
* **Infographic scripts** – these pages outline ideas for creating visual posters (family tree, “Three Leopards”, superpowers etc.) based on information from the species pages.
