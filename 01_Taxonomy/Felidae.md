# Felidae (Cat Family)

The **Felidae** are a family of carnivorous mammals that includes every species of cat, from the domestic house‑cat to the tiger.  Molecular and morphological studies divide the family into **two living sub‑families**:

* **[[Pantherinae]]** – the big‑cat branch that includes lions, tigers, leopards, jaguars, snow leopards and clouded leopards.  These cats share a flexible hyoid apparatus that allows them to roar【844804255495323†L246-L260】.  Within Pantherinae there are two genera: *Panthera* (five species) and *Neofelis* (two species).
* **[[Felinae]]** – the small‑cat branch containing all other living felids.  These cats have a rigid hyoid bone and cannot roar but can purr【844804255495323†L246-L260】.  Felinae encompasses 12 genera, including *Felis* (domestic cat), *Acinonyx* (cheetah), *Puma* (cougar), *Lynx*, *Caracal*, *Leptailurus* (serval), *Leopardus* (ocelot), and *Otocolobus* (Pallas’s cat).

Across both branches there are about **14 genera and 45 species**【844804255495323†L318-L324】.  The earliest divergence between Pantherinae and Felinae occurred over **10 million years** ago【844804255495323†L266-L279】.  Felinae then diversified into the modern small‑cat lineages during the last few million years【844804255495323†L266-L279】.

### Key features of felids

- **Carnivory:** All cats are obligate carnivores.  Their skulls house strong carnassial teeth for shearing meat and long canines for piercing.
- **Retractable claws:** Most felids have retractable claws that remain sheathed when walking, keeping them sharp.  The cheetah is a notable exception; its claws are non‑retractable and provide traction for high‑speed chases【281884566239886†L57-L66】.
- **Specialised senses:** Cats have excellent night vision, acute hearing and a developed sense of smell that aid stealth hunting.  Some species, such as the serval and the lynx, have extremely large ears that enhance hearing【300802010599608†L50-L60】【200188625492883†L41-L66】.
- **Communication:** Vocal capabilities differ between the sub‑families.  Big cats can roar but cannot purr continuously, whereas small cats can purr but cannot roar【889426918678370†L42-L50】.
