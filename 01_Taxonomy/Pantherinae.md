# Pantherinae (Big‑Cat Branch)

**Pantherinae** is the sub‑family that contains the largest living cats.  Members of this branch share a flexible hyoid apparatus that allows them to produce true roars【844804255495323†L246-L260】.  Within Pantherinae there are two extant genera:

* **Genus *Panthera*** – five species: [[Siberian Tiger]], [[African Lion]], [[Jaguar]], [[African Leopard]] and [[Snow Leopard]].  These are often called the “true big cats.”
* **Genus *Neofelis*** – the clouded leopards: [[Clouded Leopard]] and the Sunda clouded leopard (not covered in this base).  Genetic research shows that *Neofelis* split from the other Pantherinae cats more than six million years ago【452191132347828†L152-L182】.

### General characteristics

- **Roaring ability:**  The hyoid bones of pantherine cats are loosely articulated, allowing the larynx to vibrate at low frequencies and produce roaring vocalisations.  Snow leopards have a flexible hyoid but lack the full anatomy to roar; they emit a high‑pitched yowl instead【195888652515351†L129-L131】.
- **Large body size:**  Pantherine cats are among the heaviest and longest felids.  For example, the Siberian tiger can weigh up to 660 pounds (300 kg) and measure more than 10 feet (3 m) from nose to tail【736746662787535†L160-L166】.
- **Spotted coats:**  Many Panthera species have rosette patterns, though the specific shapes differ.  Jaguars have large rosettes with a central spot, while leopards have smaller, solid rosettes【898441706688837†L135-L139】.
- **Powerful build:**  Pantherine cats have muscular bodies, large heads and strong limbs suited for stalking, pouncing and subduing sizeable prey.  Jaguars, for example, have the strongest bite relative to their size of any cat【677992921894485†L30-L57】.

### Pantherine species in this knowledge base

* **[[Siberian Tiger]]** (*Panthera tigris altaica*) – largest living cat; thick coat for life in the Russian Far East【259955699961623†L75-L81】【736746662787535†L160-L166】.
* **[[African Lion]]** (*Panthera leo*) – social cat living in prides of up to 40 individuals【412797920315225†L67-L97】; second largest cat; males possess a mane.
* **[[Jaguar]]** (*Panthera onca*) – largest cat in the Americas and third largest in the world【898441706688837†L119-L124】; has a stocky build and the strongest relative bite force【677992921894485†L30-L57】.
* **[[African Leopard]]** (*Panthera pardus*) – agile climber with a wide African distribution; rosettes lack central spots【624322121786415†L115-L120】.
* **[[Snow Leopard]]** (*Panthera uncia*) – mountain‑dwelling cat of Central Asia; long tail and large paws act like snowshoes【195888652515351†L95-L101】; cannot roar【195888652515351†L129-L131】.
* **[[Clouded Leopard]]** (*Neofelis nebulosa*) – forest cat with elongated canine teeth; can climb headfirst down trees; represents an ancient branch within Pantherinae【452191132347828†L152-L182】.
