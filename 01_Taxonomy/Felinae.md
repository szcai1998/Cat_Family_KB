# Felinae (Small‑Cat Branch)

**Felinae** is the sub‑family that includes all living cats that are not members of the big‑cat genus *Panthera* or the clouded leopards.  Felinae cats share a rigid hyoid bone that enables them to **purr continuously** but prevents them from producing true roars【844804255495323†L246-L260】【889426918678370†L42-L50】.  This group encompasses a diverse collection of sizes and ecological niches, from the widely‑kept [[Domestic Cat]] to the fleet‑footed [[Cheetah]] and the high‑altitude [[Pallas’s Cat]].

### Genera within Felinae

Felinae is comprised of 12 living genera.  The ones covered in this knowledge base include:

* **Genus *Felis*** – the wildcats and [[Domestic Cat]].
* **Genus *Acinonyx*** – the [[Cheetah]].
* **Genus *Puma*** – the [[Cougar - Puma]].
* **Genus *Lynx*** – lynx species; here we cover the [[Eurasian Lynx]].
* **Genus *Caracal*** – the [[Caracal]].
* **Genus *Leptailurus*** – the [[Serval]].
* **Genus *Leopardus*** – small spotted cats of the Americas, including the [[Ocelot]].
* **Genus *Otocolobus*** – contains the [[Pallas’s Cat]].

### General characteristics

- **Purring rather than roaring:**  All Felinae cats have a single, completely ossified hyoid bone.  This rigid structure permits the vibration needed for purring but does not allow the larynx to elongate for roaring【889426918678370†L42-L50】.
- **Varied body sizes:**  Members range from the 2–7 kg domestic cat to the 90 kg cougar【889426918678370†L54-L67】.  Most felinids are small‑to‑medium size and more gracile than pantherine cats.
- **Specialised morphologies:**  Some felinids have unique adaptations: the cheetah’s non‑retractable claws and long legs enable extreme speed【281884566239886†L57-L66】; the serval has the largest ears relative to body size【300802010599608†L50-L60】; the lynx has snowshoe‑like paws for walking on snow【200188625492883†L70-L74】; and Pallas’s cats have dense fur and stocky bodies for life in extreme cold【448592063858525†L159-L170】.
- **Solitary habits:**  Most Felinae species are solitary hunters and maintain exclusive territories.  An exception is the domestic cat, which lives in close association with humans.

### Felinae species in this knowledge base

* **[[Domestic Cat]]** (*Felis catus*) – small domestic species descended from the African wildcat【925365723471490†L77-L99】; widely kept as pets around the world.
* **[[Cheetah]]** (*Acinonyx jubatus*) – fastest land animal; specialised for sprinting with non‑retractable claws and a flexible spine【281884566239886†L57-L66】.
* **[[Cougar - Puma]]** (*Puma concolor*) – large but non‑roaring cat distributed throughout the Americas; also called mountain lion; second largest cat in the Western Hemisphere【889426918678370†L54-L67】【623734751930192†L32-L39】.
* **[[Eurasian Lynx]]** (*Lynx lynx*) – medium‑sized cat of European and Asian forests; notable ear tufts and large, fur‑covered paws【200188625492883†L41-L66】【200188625492883†L70-L74】.
* **[[Caracal]]** (*Caracal caracal*) – desert and savanna cat with reddish‑gold coat and long black ear tufts; powerful jumper【774389635293037†L70-L81】.
* **[[Serval]]** (*Leptailurus serval*) – slender African cat with extremely long legs and the largest ears relative to head size【300802010599608†L50-L60】; hunts with a “wait and pounce” strategy【300802010599608†L80-L85】.
* **[[Ocelot]]** (*Leopardus pardalis*) – nocturnal spotted cat of the Americas with golden fur and rosettes【999739901386276†L70-L95】; strong climber and swimmer【999739901386276†L88-L95】.
* **[[Pallas’s Cat]]** (*Otocolobus manul*) – stocky, fluffy cat of the Central Asian steppe with dense fur for extreme temperatures; cannot meow but honks and growls【448592063858525†L159-L170】【448592063858525†L218-L223】.
